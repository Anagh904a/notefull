## ✨ What's New in v9

* **🧠 Notefull AI is stable.** The on-device AI engine has moved to Google's **LiteRT-LM** runtime for faster loading, lower memory use and reliable generation. It runs the **Gemma 4** model entirely on your device.
* **💬 New Notefull AI chat interface.** A dedicated chat screen with **full conversational support**: natural back-and-forth, follow-up questions and streamed responses.
* **📝 AI note creation.** Tell Notefull AI what you need and it creates the note for you.
* **📦 AI is an on-demand download.** The base app stays small. The AI engine and model are downloaded only when you open AI Chat and choose to start the download. Nothing downloads in the background.
* **🎨 New, robust UI.** A rebuilt interface with smoother navigation and editing, a dedicated dark theme and extensive stability fixes.
* **🔗 Notefull Connect.** Device pairing, the foundation for shared lists and collaboration in future updates.

## ⚡ Features

### 📝 Powerful Note Taking
* Create and manage notes effortlessly with a fast, responsive editor.
* Build checklists for tasks and everyday activities.
* Instant search across your content.
* Clean UI, smooth navigation and a dedicated dark theme, designed for distraction-free productivity.

### 🧠 Notefull AI
A conversational assistant, powered by **Gemma 4** and running directly on your device.

* **Natural conversations:** chat through a familiar, dedicated interface to explore ideas and ask questions.
* **AI note creation:** ask the AI to create notes from your instructions.
* **On-device intelligence:** all AI processing happens locally.
* **Privacy-focused:** your AI conversations are not sent to external AI servers.
* **Free:** no API keys, subscriptions or usage caps.

> **Current limitations:** Notefull AI cannot yet reason over your existing notes and lists. It can chat and create notes. Working with your own data, more AI tools and agentic capabilities are planned for future updates, along with an option to completely disable Notefull AI.

**AI is optional.** The model is not part of the initial installation. A download of about **2.4 GB** starts only after you open AI Chat and start it yourself. Wi-Fi is recommended, because mobile data charges may apply. Performance varies by device.

### 🔒 Privacy & Security
* Password-protected notes.
* Biometric authentication.
* Privacy screen when switching apps.
* No account or registration.
* No ads, tracking or analytics.
* Local-first data storage and no mandatory cloud sync.

### 🔗 Notefull Connect – Device Pairing
Connect supported devices with Notefull Connect. It is the foundation for a connected productivity experience, with shared lists and collaborative features planned for future updates.

### ⏰ Reminders & Notifications
* Set reminders for notes and tasks and receive timely notifications.
* Powered by local Android alarms, with no push servers.
* *Android battery optimization may affect notification delivery.*

### 🗑️ Trash – Recover Deleted Notes
Restore accidentally deleted notes whenever you need to from the built-in Trash.

### ⚡ Offline-First
Create and manage notes with no internet connection. AI model downloads and certain connectivity features are the only things that need internet access.

## 📱 Requirements

| Component | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Notefull (notes, lists, reminders)** | Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer |
| **Notefull AI** | Android 10+, 64-bit ARM device, 4+ CPU cores, 4 GB+ RAM |
| **Storage for AI** | About 2.4 GB free for the model, plus the small AI engine module |

Devices that do not meet the AI requirements still get the full notes and lists app.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy Principles

* **Zero tracking:** no tracking SDKs, telemetry, analytics or ad libraries.
* **Local-first:** notes, lists and metadata live on your device.
* **No lock-in:** open the app and start writing. No login needed.
* **Private AI:** models run on-device, and your AI conversations stay on your phone.
